Mushroom Foray
Spore Print Matcher

Read the spore print before you read the field guide.

Match a spore print photo to the closest of six colour classes, grade a lookalike warning score from cap width and gill crowding, and keep every find in a named foray session.

  • Colour match in CIE Lab with delta-E and a confidence percentage.
  • Lookalike score that explains both of its weighted factors.
  • Foray log of swatch, confidence, habitat and risk band, charted per session.

Heuristic only. This tool never identifies a species and never says a mushroom is safe to eat.
